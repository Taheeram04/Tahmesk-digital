Last updated: July 29, 2026

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide directly: your first name, email address, and message. We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or other sensitive personal data through this site.

Contact form submissions are stored in our secure database and are only accessible to authorized Tahmesk Digital staff. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
